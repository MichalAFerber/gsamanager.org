GSA Manager is a cloud-based business management application hosted on Microsoft
Azure. Actively developed since 2007 and still trusted by its very first
customer, it brings point of sale, inventory, CRM, task management and payment
processing into one application.

[Install GSA Manager](install/publish.htm)

---

## What it does

- **Point of sale** — register, receipts and cash-drawer support.
- **Inventory** — stock levels, labels and barcode scanning.
- **CRM and memberships** — contacts, membership records and renewals.
- **Checklists and tasks** — recurring work tracked against the day.
- **Payments** — card processing through Authorize.Net or Braintree.

## Requirements

Windows, with:

- [.NET Framework 4.8](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2088631)
- [Crystal Reports for Visual Studio SP30 Runtime 32-bit MSI](https://origin-az.softwaredownloads.sap.com/public/file/0020000000195592021)
- [DYMO Label Software 8.7.4](https://s3.amazonaws.com/download.dymo.com/dymo/Software/Win/DLS8Setup8.7.4.exe)
- [GSA Support Files](GSA%20Manager.zip)

Supported hardware includes
[APG cash drawers](https://www.cashdrawer.com/software-drivers-developers/),
[Dymo label printers](https://www.dymo.com/en-US) and
[Zebra barcode scanners](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/products/scanners/general-purpose-scanners/handheld/ls2208.html).
